About

I'm Olena — a product photographer and retoucher from Ukraine.

I work with beauty, fragrance, jewellery and fashion brands — from the first light test in the studio to the final pixel of retouch. Every frame is built around one idea: the object should look exactly the way it feels in your hands.

Glass that holds the light, metal without a single stray reflection, powder you can almost smell, colour that matches the packaging. I shoot and retouch myself, so the image is planned as a whole from the very beginning.
